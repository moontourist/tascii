Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

tascii is a to-do list extension for Chrome. It collects no personal data, and nothing you type into it is sent anywhere.

Your tasks, lists and settings are saved on your own computer with Chrome's local storage. They aren't uploaded, synced, sold or shared, and the developer can't see them. Removing the extension deletes them.

The extension uses two permissions. The side panel permission shows the list in Chrome's side panel. The storage permission saves your tasks. It has no access to the websites you visit and makes no network requests. Its only link out is the GitHub button, which opens in a new tab when you click it.

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. The demo on the home page saves its tasks in your browser's local storage, on your computer only.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
