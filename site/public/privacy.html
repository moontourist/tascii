Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

tascii is a to-do list extension for Chrome. It collects no personal data, and nothing you type into it is sent anywhere, unless you connect it to a tascii server you run yourself.

Your tasks, lists and settings are saved on your own computer with Chrome's local storage. They aren't uploaded, synced, sold or shared, and the developer can't see them. Removing the extension deletes them.

Your own server, if you want one: tascii can also keep your list on a tascii server you host yourself (on your own computer or home network), so your phone and other computers share it. This is off unless you type the server's address into prefs. Chrome then asks you to allow that one address. From then on, the extension sends your list, and the password if you entered one, to that address only. The developer never sees it. Empty the address to go back to the list saved on your computer, which the extension leaves untouched.

The extension uses two permissions, plus one more only if you connect your own server. The side panel permission shows the list in Chrome's side panel. The storage permission saves your tasks. It has no access to the websites you visit. If you connect your own server, Chrome asks you to let it reach that one address, and it makes no other network requests. Its only link out is the GitHub button, which opens in a new tab when you click it.

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. The demo on the home page saves its tasks in your browser's local storage, on your computer only.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
